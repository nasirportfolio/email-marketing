How to Write a Shipping Delay Email to Customers

A shipping delay email tells a customer that their shipment is taking longer than expected and explains what they can reasonably expect next. The best messages are direct, specific, and focused on the customer's order rather than the company's internal problems.

Quick Answer:

A good shipping delay email should identify the affected order, clearly explain that shipping is delayed, provide the latest reliable delivery information, explain what happens next, and give the customer a way to get help if needed. If you do not have a confirmed new date, say so instead of promising one.

Shipping Delay Email Template

If you need a customer-ready message quickly, adapt this template using accurate order and shipping information.

Subject: Update on your shipment for order #1847

Hi [Customer Name],

We wanted to let you know that your order #1847 is experiencing a shipping delay.

Your package was originally expected to arrive around [Original Date]. The latest update indicates that delivery is now expected around [New Date], subject to the carrier's schedule.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. You can contact us at [Support Email] if you have questions about your order.

Thank you,
[Business Name] Customer Support

What Is a Shipping Delay Email?

A shipping delay email is a customer communication sent when an order's shipment is taking longer than originally expected.

The delay may occur before a package leaves the fulfillment operation or after it has been handed to a carrier. The exact explanation should match what the business actually knows about the shipment.

This makes a shipping delay email different from a general order delay message. A shipping delay focuses specifically on the shipment and its delivery progress.

When Should You Send a Shipping Delay Email?

Send the message when you have enough reliable information to tell the customer that the expected shipping or delivery timeline has changed.

Avoid sending repeated messages every time a tracking system receives a minor status change. Instead, communicate when the change is meaningful to the customer's expectations.

If the carrier has confirmed a significant delay, the customer should not have to discover the problem by repeatedly checking the tracking page.

What to Include in a Shipping Delay Email

The customer should be able to understand the situation without contacting support for basic information.

  • Customer name, when appropriate
  • Order number
  • Relevant product or shipment information
  • Clear statement that shipping is delayed
  • Latest reliable delivery estimate, if available
  • Tracking information, when useful
  • What happens next
  • Customer support contact information

Do not include information that you cannot verify. A precise delivery date is useful only when there is a reasonable basis for providing it.

How to Write a Shipping Delay Email Step by Step

1. State the delay clearly

Tell the customer immediately that the shipment is delayed. Avoid opening with vague language such as "There has been an update regarding your order."

Clear opening:

Your order #1847 is experiencing a shipping delay, and its delivery will take longer than originally expected.

2. Identify the affected order

Include the order number so the customer can quickly connect the message to the correct purchase.

3. Give the latest reliable information

If you have a confirmed or reasonably reliable new delivery estimate, provide it. If the carrier has not provided one, explain that clearly.

4. Explain the next step

Tell the customer whether the shipment is still moving, whether another update will be sent, or whether they should contact support.

5. Keep the apology proportional

A brief apology can acknowledge the inconvenience. The main purpose of the email, however, is to provide useful information.

How to Explain a New Delivery Estimate

When a new delivery estimate is available, make it easy to understand. For example, you might say:

Your package is now expected to arrive by October 8.

If the date comes from a carrier estimate rather than a guarantee from your business, make that distinction clear.

Based on the latest carrier update, your package is currently expected to arrive by October 8.

Avoid presenting an estimate as a guaranteed delivery date when circumstances outside your control can still change it.

What If There Is No New Delivery Date?

Sometimes a carrier or fulfillment partner has not provided a dependable new date. In that situation, honest uncertainty is better than false precision.

Example:

We do not have a confirmed delivery date yet. We are monitoring the shipment and will provide another update when reliable information becomes available.

This approach helps prevent a second customer-service problem caused by promising a date that later changes.

Shipping Delay Email Subject Lines

The subject line should make the purpose of the email obvious without sounding unnecessarily alarming.

  • Update on your shipment for order #1847
  • Shipping update for your order
  • Important update about your delivery
  • Your order delivery has been delayed
  • New delivery update for order #1847
  • Update: Your package is taking longer than expected

Including the order number can make the message easier for customers to recognize, particularly when they have several recent purchases.

U.S. Ecommerce Example

Imagine a U.S. customer orders a $64.99 product and receives an original delivery estimate of October 5. The carrier later reports that the package is delayed and is now expected around October 8.

A useful email would clearly state the delay, identify the order, provide the updated estimate, and explain that the date is based on the latest available shipping information.

The exact wording should reflect the store's actual shipping policy and the information provided by the carrier.

Should You Include Tracking Information?

If a tracking number or tracking page is already available, including it can help customers verify the latest shipment status themselves.

Keep the tracking information easy to find. The customer should not have to search through several paragraphs to locate it.

Do not create a tracking link that does not work or send customers to an unrelated page. The destination should correspond to the actual shipment.

Automating Shipping Delay Emails

Shipping delay notifications can be connected to order-management, fulfillment, or shipping systems so that customers receive updates when meaningful shipment events occur.

Automation works best when the trigger uses reliable order or carrier data. Sending a message every time a minor tracking event changes can create unnecessary email volume.

Common Shipping Delay Email Mistakes

Being vague

Customers need to know whether their shipment is delayed. Avoid generic language that hides the actual status.

Promising an unconfirmed date

Do not turn a carrier estimate into a guaranteed delivery promise unless your business can genuinely guarantee it.

Blaming the carrier unnecessarily

If a carrier caused the delay, you can explain the relevant fact without turning the email into a complaint about the carrier.

Leaving out the order number

Customers should be able to identify the affected order immediately.

Repeating information the customer already knows

A delay email should focus on what changed and what the customer needs to know now.

Sending updates without meaningful information

Avoid sending repeated emails simply to increase communication frequency. Each message should provide a useful status change or next step.

Shipping Delay Email Checklist

  • State clearly that the shipment is delayed.
  • Include the correct order number.
  • Identify the affected shipment or product.
  • Provide the latest reliable delivery estimate.
  • Clearly communicate uncertainty if no date is confirmed.
  • Include tracking information when useful and available.
  • Explain what happens next.
  • Use a clear and specific subject line.
  • Keep the apology brief and appropriate.
  • Make customer support easy to reach.
  • Verify order and shipment information before sending.
  • Use meaningful triggers for automated delay notifications.

Final Thoughts

A shipping delay email should reduce uncertainty, not create more of it. Tell customers what changed, identify their order, provide the latest reliable information, and explain what happens next.

If you do not have a confirmed delivery date, say so. Clear and honest communication is more useful than a precise promise that may need to be corrected later.
