How to Write a Missed Delivery Email to Customers

A missed delivery email informs a customer that a delivery attempt was unsuccessful and explains what happens next. A useful message identifies the order, shares verified delivery information, and gives the customer a practical next step without assigning blame.

Quick answer:

A good missed delivery email confirms the unsuccessful attempt, includes the order number, provides a verified tracking link, explains available redelivery or pickup options, and gives the customer a way to contact support. Only include dates, reasons, and options that are confirmed for that shipment.

Missed Delivery Email Template

Adapt this template to the real order details and the latest carrier information before sending it.

Subject: Delivery update for order [Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We wanted to let you know that a delivery attempt for order [Order Number] could not be completed.

The latest tracking information shows: [Confirmed Delivery Status]. You can check the shipment here: [Verified Tracking Link].

[If confirmed: Another delivery attempt is expected on Date.] If the tracking page does not explain your available options, contact us at [Support Email] and include your order number.

We are sorry for the inconvenience and will be happy to help you review the available next steps.

Thank you,
[Business Name] Customer Support

What Is a Missed Delivery Email?

A missed delivery email is a transactional or service message sent after a delivery attempt cannot be completed. Depending on the carrier's confirmed status, the reason may involve access restrictions, a signature requirement, an incomplete address, or another delivery condition.

The email should distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions. If the carrier has not provided a reason, explain that the attempt was unsuccessful without guessing what happened.

When Should You Send It?

Send the message after the carrier or shipping system records a failed delivery attempt and reliable status information is available. Timely communication can reduce confusion, but sending too early can create unnecessary concern if the tracking event is incorrect or temporary.

If the carrier already sends a detailed notification, consider whether your store email adds useful order context, support information, or instructions rather than simply repeating the same alert.

What Information Should It Include?

  • The correct order number and shipment identification.
  • A clear statement that the delivery attempt was unsuccessful.
  • The attempt date, if useful and confirmed.
  • The reason for the unsuccessful attempt, if the carrier confirms it.
  • The next expected delivery date, only when reliable.
  • A verified tracking link.
  • Available redelivery or pickup instructions.
  • A working customer support contact.

Include only the information relevant to that customer's shipment. Avoid adding unnecessary personal information or making the customer search through unrelated details to understand what to do.

How to Write a Missed Delivery Email

1. Use a clear subject line

Make the purpose immediately understandable. If appropriate, include the order number so the customer can identify the purchase.

2. Explain the status in plain language

Example:

A delivery attempt for order [Order Number] could not be completed today.

3. Give the confirmed reason, if known

If the carrier reports that a signature was required and nobody was available to sign, you can explain that fact. Do not state that the customer was absent unless the available information actually supports it.

4. Explain the next step

Tell the customer whether another attempt is expected, pickup is available, or they need to consult the carrier's instructions. If the status is unclear, say that it is being checked instead of inventing a solution.

5. Make support easy to reach

Provide a working support email or contact page. Ask the customer to include the order number so the support team can find the correct shipment more efficiently.

Missed Delivery Email Subject Line Examples

  • Delivery update for order [Order Number]
  • We couldn't complete your delivery
  • Missed delivery: order [Order Number]
  • Update about your package delivery
  • Next steps for your delivery
  • Action may be needed for your delivery

Use an action-oriented subject only when the customer genuinely needs to do something. Avoid alarming wording or false urgency.

Redelivery and Pickup Options

The appropriate next step depends on the carrier, destination, shipment type, and store policy. Available options may include:

  • Waiting for a confirmed second delivery attempt.
  • Requesting or scheduling redelivery where supported.
  • Collecting the package from an authorized pickup point.
  • Updating delivery instructions through an official carrier service.
  • Contacting the store or carrier for help.

Do not promise that an option is available until it has been verified for the specific shipment. If pickup is offered, provide the confirmed location and collection deadline, when available.

Template When Customer Action Is Needed

Subject: Action may be needed for order [Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

The carrier has reported that the delivery attempt for order [Order Number] could not be completed. The latest status is: [Confirmed Carrier Reason or Status].

Please review the official tracking page for available next steps: [Verified Tracking Link]. If the carrier asks you to choose a delivery option or provide additional information, follow the instructions shown there.

If you cannot determine what to do next, contact us at [Support Email] and include your order number. We will help you review the options available under our delivery policy.

Thank you,
[Business Name] Customer Support

Before sending: Replace every bracketed field, remove any instruction that does not apply, and test the tracking and support links. Never send sample order numbers or placeholder links to real customers.

Automating Missed Delivery Notifications

A store can automate missed delivery messages when its order-management or shipping platform receives a reliable failed-delivery event. Automation can improve consistency, but only when the trigger reflects the actual shipment status.

Useful safeguards include checking the event before sending, preventing duplicate notifications, using the correct order data, and avoiding unconfirmed delivery dates. Review messages when a carrier status is ambiguous or changes shortly after the initial event.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Blaming the customer

Do not assume the customer caused the failed delivery. Explain the verified status in neutral language.

Providing an unconfirmed date

Do not promise a delivery date unless the carrier's information supports it. When the date is unknown, provide the tracking link and explain how the customer can get an update.

Leaving out the next step

A message that only announces a failed attempt leaves the customer uncertain. Include a relevant next action or explain where reliable instructions can be found.

Sending duplicate alerts

Coordinate store notifications with carrier notifications where possible. Send a separate message when it adds meaningful information or support.

Using broken links or placeholders

Test the tracking destination and support contact. Confirm that all order-specific fields are populated before an automated message is sent.

Missed Delivery Email Pre-Send Checklist

  • Confirm that the delivery attempt actually failed.
  • Check the correct order number and shipment.
  • Use a clear, neutral explanation.
  • Include the reason only if confirmed.
  • Give a delivery date only if reliable.
  • Explain available redelivery or pickup options.
  • Test the tracking and support links.
  • Remove all sample values and placeholders.
  • Check the message on mobile devices.
  • Prevent duplicate or incorrect automated messages.

Final Thoughts

A missed delivery email should turn an unsuccessful delivery attempt into a clear next step. Identify the order, explain the confirmed status, provide useful tracking information, and make support easy to contact.

Keep the message accurate and respectful. Reliable information and practical instructions help customers understand their options without creating false expectations.
