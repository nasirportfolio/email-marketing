How to Write a Backorder Cancellation Email to Customers

Customer service email templates · Practical writing guide

Sometimes a business cannot replenish an item that customers have already ordered. When an order or backordered item must be cancelled, customers deserve a clear explanation, accurate information about their payment, and practical instructions for what happens next.

A backorder cancellation email should not leave customers wondering whether their order is still active or whether they need to contact support to get an answer. This guide provides ready-to-adapt templates and explains how to communicate cancellation decisions respectfully.

Quick Answer: What Should a Backorder Cancellation Email Include?

  • The customer's order number and affected product.
  • A direct statement explaining what is being cancelled.
  • A brief, truthful reason for the cancellation.
  • The actual status of any refund or payment reversal, if applicable.
  • Any remaining items that will still be fulfilled.
  • Available alternatives or support contact details.

Keep the email clear and considerate. Do not state that a refund has been issued unless your payment or order system confirms it.

Backorder Cancellation Email Template

General cancellation template

Subject: An Update About Your Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We are contacting you about [Product Name] in order #[Order Number]. Unfortunately, we are unable to obtain the stock needed to fulfil this part of your order, so we need to cancel [the item / the order].

We understand that this is disappointing, especially after you placed your order expecting to receive the item. We apologize for the inconvenience.

[Insert the accurate payment or refund status here. For example: “Our records show that your refund of [Amount] was initiated on [Date].” Use this sentence only when verified.]

If you would like help finding an alternative, please contact us at [Support Email] or [Support Phone]. We will be happy to explain the options available.

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Customize the wording to match the actual situation. If only one product is cancelled, do not imply that the entire order has been cancelled.

1. What Is a Backorder Cancellation Email?

A backorder cancellation email tells a customer that an item awaiting replenishment, or the order containing that item, will no longer be fulfilled. It confirms the decision and explains what the customer should expect next.

A backorder update email has a different purpose. It tells the customer that the item is still expected to become available and provides the latest information. A cancellation email communicates that the item or order will not proceed under the current arrangement.

Cancellation may happen because a supplier cannot provide more stock, the product has been discontinued, inventory records were inaccurate, or the customer has requested cancellation. The email should explain the actual reason rather than use a generic explanation that may not fit the situation.

It is also important to distinguish cancelling an order from confirming a refund. Those are separate events. State the cancellation status and payment status accurately.

2. Common Reasons for Cancelling a Backordered Item

The supplier cannot replenish the product

If your supplier confirms that the item cannot be supplied, explain that you cannot fulfil the order as expected. Avoid blaming another business unnecessarily; the customer needs a clear explanation and a practical next step.

The product has been discontinued

If the manufacturer has stopped producing the item, tell the customer that it is no longer available for fulfilment. If suitable alternatives exist, offer them without implying that the customer must accept one.

The inventory estimate was incorrect

If the product was listed as available or expected sooner than it actually could be supplied, acknowledge the error. A straightforward explanation is more helpful than hiding the mistake behind vague wording.

The customer requested cancellation

If the customer no longer wishes to wait, confirm what was cancelled, explain the next steps, and provide the verified payment status.

Writing tip: Explain the reason in one or two useful sentences. Customers generally need to understand the decision and its consequences more than they need a lengthy account of internal operations.

3. Backorder Cancellation Email Subject Line Examples

Choose a subject line that is clear without sounding alarming.

  • Update About Your Order #[Order Number]
  • Important Information About [Product Name]
  • Cancellation Notice for [Product Name]
  • An Update on Your Backordered Item
  • Your Order #[Order Number]: Cancellation Details
  • Information About the Availability of [Product Name]
  • Confirmation of Your Requested Order Cancellation
  • Next Steps for Your Cancelled Item

If the customer requested cancellation, say so in the subject when appropriate. If the business is initiating cancellation, avoid wording that incorrectly suggests the customer requested it.

4. Email Template When Stock Cannot Be Replenished

Use this version when the business cannot obtain the item and must cancel it. Adjust the wording to reflect your actual fulfilment policy and the payment status.

Template: Unable to fulfil the item

Subject: Cancellation Notice for [Product Name] — Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We have an update about [Product Name] in order #[Order Number]. Unfortunately, we have been unable to secure the stock required to fulfil your order, and we cannot provide a reliable replenishment date.

For this reason, we need to cancel [Product Name] from your order. We understand that this is not the outcome you expected, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

Regarding your payment, [insert the confirmed payment or refund status]. If you have questions about the cancellation, please contact us at [Support Email].

If you would like to consider a similar product, we can explain any alternatives currently available.

Thank you for giving us the opportunity to assist you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

5. Email Template When the Customer Requests Cancellation

When a customer asks to cancel because of a long wait, acknowledge the request and confirm the action only after it has actually been completed in your order system.

Template: Customer-requested cancellation

Subject: Your Cancellation Request for Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us about the backordered [Product Name] in order #[Order Number].

We have [cancelled the item / submitted your cancellation request for processing]. The current status is [verified status].

Your payment status is [verified payment or refund information]. We will share any further confirmation when it becomes available through our order or payment system.

We understand that waiting longer may not work for you, and we appreciate you letting us know your preference. If you need help with another order, contact us at [Support Email].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Do not describe a request as completed if it is still awaiting approval or processing. The email should match the order system's actual status.

6. Email Template for Cancelling One Item Only

If an order contains several products, make it clear whether the cancellation affects one item or the entire order. This avoids unnecessary concern and reduces support questions.

Template: Partial order cancellation

Subject: Update on One Item in Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We are writing about order #[Order Number]. Unfortunately, [Product Name] is no longer available for us to fulfil, so we have cancelled that item.

The other items in your order are [still being processed / already shipped / in another verified status]. This cancellation applies only to [Product Name], not to the rest of your order.

The payment or refund status for the cancelled item is [insert confirmed details]. If you have questions about this change, please contact [Support Contact].

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

Use the sentence about the remaining items only after checking their current status. If another item is also delayed, explain that separately rather than implying the whole order is progressing normally.

7. How to Explain Refund Status in a Cancellation Email

Customers often want to know what will happen to the money they paid. Answer that question directly, but distinguish between a cancellation being recorded, a refund being initiated, and a refund appearing in the customer's account.

If the refund has been initiated

State the amount and initiation date if those details have been confirmed. Avoid guaranteeing when the money will appear unless your payment provider gives a reliable, applicable timeframe.

If the refund has not been initiated

Explain the current status and the next step. Do not use “your refund has been processed” when the payment system shows that it is still pending.

If the customer has not paid

If no payment was captured, explain that there is no captured payment to refund, if your records confirm this. Where an authorization hold exists, explain its status accurately rather than describing it as a completed refund.

Example refund-status wording

  • Refund initiated: “Our records show that a refund of [Amount] was initiated on [Date].”
  • Refund pending: “Your refund request is currently being processed. We will update you when its status changes.”
  • Payment not captured: “Our records show that no payment was captured for this item.”
  • Status being checked: “We are checking the payment status and will follow up by [Date].”

Use these examples only when they accurately reflect your payment records. Follow your store's policies and applicable consumer-protection rules when communicating refunds and cancellations.

8. How to Communicate Cancellation with Empathy

A customer may have chosen a product carefully or needed it for a particular occasion. A cancellation message should recognize the inconvenience without becoming defensive or overly dramatic.

  • Be direct: State clearly whether the item or order is cancelled.
  • Be specific: Identify the product and order number.
  • Be honest: Give the real reason at an appropriate level of detail.
  • Be accountable: Apologize when the business has caused inconvenience.
  • Be helpful: Explain refund status, alternatives, and how to reach support.
  • Respect the customer's choice: Do not pressure them to select a substitute.

Example: A concise, considerate message

Subject: An Update About Your Backordered Item

Hi Alex,

Unfortunately, we can no longer obtain the [Product Name] you ordered in order #[Order Number]. We have therefore cancelled that item. We know this is disappointing, and we apologize for the inconvenience.

Your current payment status is [verified status]. If you would like help considering an alternative, our team is available at [Support Email].

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
[Company Name] Support Team

This is an illustrative example. Replace the names, order details, and payment status with accurate information before sending.

9. Common Backorder Cancellation Email Mistakes

1. Hiding the cancellation in vague language

Phrases such as “there is an issue with your order” do not clearly explain the decision. State which item or order is cancelled.

2. Promising a refund that has not been confirmed

A cancellation does not automatically prove that a refund has been issued. Check the payment record before describing its status.

3. Failing to distinguish a partial cancellation

If only one item is affected, explicitly say what happens to the remaining items, after verifying their status.

4. Blaming a supplier without explaining the next step

The cause may be relevant, but customers also need to know what happens to their order and payment. Keep the focus on useful information.

5. Offering alternatives that are not available

Check current inventory before recommending a substitute. An unavailable alternative can create another frustrating experience.

6. Using the wrong email automation

A cancelled order should not continue receiving ordinary backorder reminders. Update the customer's communication status when the cancellation is recorded.

7. Forgetting the support contact

Include a working support email, phone number, or help link so the customer can ask questions about the cancellation.

10. Backorder Cancellation Email Checklist

Before sending the message, verify the following:

  • The subject line accurately describes the message.
  • The order number and affected product are correct.
  • The cancellation is clearly stated.
  • The reason is truthful and appropriate to share.
  • The message distinguishes an item cancellation from a full order cancellation.
  • The status of other items has been checked, if relevant.
  • The payment and refund wording matches the payment system.
  • Any alternative offered is genuinely available.
  • The customer has a working way to contact support.
  • The email is readable on a mobile screen.
  • Automated backorder reminders will stop when appropriate.
  • The email contains no unsupported guarantees or misleading claims.

Final Thoughts

A backorder cancellation email should give customers a clear answer: what has been cancelled, why the business cannot fulfil the item, and what happens next.

Use accurate order and payment information, explain available options without pressure, and make it easy for customers to contact support. A clear message cannot remove every disappointment, but it can prevent avoidable confusion and help preserve trust.
