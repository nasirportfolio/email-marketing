How to Write a Backorder Refund Email to Customers

Customer service email templates · Practical writing guide

When a backordered item cannot be supplied or a customer chooses not to wait, a refund may be necessary. A clear backorder refund email explains the amount involved, the reason for the refund, its current status, and what the customer should expect next.

Refund communication needs to be accurate as well as considerate. A cancellation notice does not necessarily mean that the refund has already been initiated, and an initiated refund may not yet appear in the customer's account. This guide shows how to explain these situations without making promises your payment system cannot verify.

Quick Answer: What Should a Backorder Refund Email Include?

  • The customer's name and order number.
  • The product or item being refunded.
  • The refund amount and currency, when confirmed.
  • The actual refund status: pending, initiated, or completed in your system.
  • The payment method, if known and appropriate to disclose.
  • A realistic explanation of what happens next.
  • A support contact for questions or discrepancies.

Check the order and payment records before sending. Never claim that money has been refunded simply because an item or order was cancelled.

Backorder Refund Email Template

General refund confirmation

Subject: Refund Update for Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We are writing about [Product Name] in order #[Order Number]. Because we are unable to fulfil this item while it is on backorder, we have arranged a refund for [Amount and Currency].

Our records show that the refund was [initiated / submitted for processing] on [Date] to [Payment Method, if confirmed].

The time it takes for the refund to appear may depend on your payment provider. We cannot confirm the posting date unless it is provided by the relevant payment service.

If you have questions about this refund, contact us at [Support Email] and include order #[Order Number].

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Use this template only when the stated refund amount and status have been verified. If the refund has not yet been initiated, use a pending status message instead.

1. What Is a Backorder Refund Email?

A backorder refund email explains a refund connected to an item that cannot be supplied as expected, or an order the customer has chosen not to keep waiting for.

The email may confirm that a refund has been initiated, explain that it is still being processed, or provide a status update after a customer asks where their money is.

It differs from a backorder cancellation email. A cancellation email confirms that an item or order will not proceed. A refund email concentrates on the payment outcome and what the customer should expect regarding their money.

These events can happen close together, but they are not identical. Keep the cancellation status and refund status separate in both your order system and your customer communication.

2. Backorder Refund Email Subject Line Examples

Use a subject line that makes the purpose of the message easy to recognize.

  • Refund Update for Order #[Order Number]
  • Refund Confirmation for [Product Name]
  • An Update on Your Backorder Refund
  • Your Refund Request: Order #[Order Number]
  • Refund Status for Your Cancelled Item
  • Payment Update for [Product Name]
  • We Have an Update About Your Refund
  • Partial Refund Details for Order #[Order Number]

Use “refund confirmation” only when your system confirms the relevant refund action. If the refund is still under review or awaiting processing, “refund update” is more accurate.

3. Email Template When the Refund Has Been Initiated

Use this template when the payment system confirms that the refund request has been submitted or initiated. Do not confuse that status with confirmation that the customer's bank has posted the funds.

Template: Refund initiated

Subject: Refund Confirmation for Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We are sorry that we could not fulfil [Product Name] from order #[Order Number]. We have initiated a refund of [Amount and Currency] on [Date].

The refund was submitted to [Original Payment Method, if confirmed]. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on the payment provider and your financial institution.

You do not need to submit another refund request for this transaction unless our support team advises you otherwise.

If you have questions, please contact [Support Email] and reference order #[Order Number].

Thank you for your patience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

4. Email Template When the Refund Is Still Pending

Sometimes the cancellation is recorded before the payment team or payment provider has completed the refund process. Explain the actual status and, where possible, tell the customer when you will check again.

Template: Refund pending

Subject: Update on Your Pending Refund — Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We are following up about the refund for [Product Name] in order #[Order Number]. Your cancellation has been recorded, but the refund is still pending in our system.

Our team is checking the payment status and will provide another update by [Date]. We do not want to give you an inaccurate confirmation before the refund action is verified.

We apologize for the wait. If you have questions in the meantime, contact us at [Support Email].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Include a follow-up date only if your team can meet it. If the delay is caused by a payment provider, explain what is known without assigning an unsupported completion date.

5. Email Template for a Partial Backorder Refund

If an order contains several items but only one cannot be supplied, explain the amount being refunded and the status of the remaining items. This helps prevent customers from assuming the entire order has been cancelled.

Template: One item refunded

Subject: Partial Refund Update for Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

We have an update about order #[Order Number]. Unfortunately, we could not fulfil [Product Name], so we have arranged a refund of [Amount and Currency] for that item.

The other items in your order are [insert their verified status]. This refund applies to [Product Name] only.

The refund status is [verified status], and the payment method is [method, if confirmed]. If you need help understanding the adjustment, contact us at [Support Email].

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

Verify the amount carefully. A partial refund should not accidentally be described as a full order refund.

6. Email Template When a Customer Requests a Backorder Refund

When a customer contacts support because they no longer want to wait, acknowledge the request and explain whether it has been approved, completed, or is still being reviewed.

Template: Customer refund request

Subject: Update on Your Refund Request — Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us about the backordered [Product Name] in order #[Order Number]. We understand that you no longer wish to wait for the item.

Your request is currently [approved / under review / completed], according to our latest records.

The refund amount is [Amount and Currency], and its current payment status is [verified status]. We will update you if any further action is needed.

If you have questions, please reply to this email or contact us at [Support Email].

Thank you for giving us the opportunity to assist you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Do not say a refund has been approved if the request is still awaiting review. Use the exact status shown in your system.

7. What to Say When the Refund Is Not Visible Yet

A customer may contact you because the refund does not appear in their account. Start by checking the payment record rather than assuming the refund is complete or that the customer is mistaken.

  1. Confirm the order number and the refund amount.
  2. Check whether the refund was submitted, accepted, rejected, or remains pending.
  3. Verify the payment method and any available transaction reference.
  4. Explain the status in plain language.
  5. Give a realistic next step, including contacting the payment provider if appropriate.

Template: Customer cannot see the refund

Subject: We're Checking Your Refund — Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for letting us know that you cannot see the refund for order #[Order Number]. We understand why you would want to check its status.

Our current records show that the refund is [verified status]. We are checking the available transaction details and will contact you by [Date] with an update.

If the payment provider has supplied a transaction reference, we will share it where appropriate so that you can make further enquiries if necessary.

We apologize for the uncertainty and appreciate your patience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

Do not promise that the refund will arrive on a particular day unless that timing is supported by reliable information for the customer's payment method.

8. How to Write a Clear Backorder Refund Email

Start with the reason for writing

Tell the customer that the message concerns a refund for a specific order or item. Avoid a long introduction that delays the important information.

Identify the product and order

Include the order number and product name. For a partial refund, state exactly which item the refund covers.

State the amount and currency

If the amount is confirmed, provide it in the currency used for the transaction. If the amount is not yet confirmed, say that it is being checked rather than guessing.

Use precise status language

Distinguish between a refund request being received, a refund being approved, a refund being initiated, and the funds being posted to the customer's account. Use the terms that match your actual process.

Give a useful next step

Explain whether the customer needs to do anything, whether your team will follow up, and how to contact support. Avoid leaving the customer to guess whether another request is required.

Practical tip: Make the email answer three questions: How much is being refunded? What is its current status? What happens next?

9. Common Backorder Refund Email Mistakes

1. Saying “refunded” before the refund is initiated

Cancellation and refund processing may be separate steps. Verify the payment record before confirming the refund.

2. Omitting the refund amount

When the amount is confirmed, include it so the customer can reconcile the transaction with their order and payment records.

3. Promising an unsupported arrival date

The time for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider and financial institution. Use only reliable timing information that applies to the transaction.

4. Confusing a partial refund with a full refund

Identify the affected item and the amount being returned. Explain the status of other items only after checking the order.

5. Ignoring a customer follow-up

If the refund is still pending, provide an update when promised. Even if there is no new information, tell the customer what has been checked and when you will follow up again.

6. Asking the customer to repeat information unnecessarily

Include the order number and relevant transaction details in support communications so the customer does not have to explain the entire situation again.

7. Using automated messages after the case is resolved

Stop pending-refund reminders or backorder notices when the order status changes. Automation should reflect the latest verified payment information.

10. Backorder Refund Email Checklist

  • The subject line clearly describes the refund update.
  • The customer's name and order number are correct.
  • The refunded product or items are identified accurately.
  • The amount and currency have been verified.
  • The refund status matches the payment system.
  • The payment method is mentioned only when confirmed and appropriate.
  • Any estimated timing is supported by reliable information.
  • The message explains the next step for the customer.
  • A working support contact is included.
  • The email is readable on mobile devices.
  • Automated follow-ups will stop or change when the case is resolved.
  • The email contains no unsupported guarantees.

Final Thoughts

A useful backorder refund email is specific, accurate, and easy to understand. Identify the order and item, state the amount when confirmed, explain the actual refund status, and give the customer a clear next step.

Most importantly, do not confuse a cancelled item with a completed refund. Keeping those statuses accurate helps customers understand what has happened and gives your support team a clearer way to resolve questions.
