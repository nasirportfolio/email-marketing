How to Write a Backorder Shipping Confirmation Email to Customers

Customer service email templates · Practical writing guide

When a backordered product finally ships, customers should receive a clear confirmation explaining that the item is on its way. A useful email identifies the product, confirms the shipment, provides tracking information when available, and explains what to expect next.

After waiting for an item to become available, customers need more than a generic shipping notification. They need to know that the delayed product has actually left the fulfilment process and that they can follow its progress.

Quick Answer: What Should a Backorder Shipping Confirmation Include?

  • The customer's name and order number.
  • The backordered item that has shipped.
  • The actual shipment date, when confirmed.
  • The carrier and tracking number, if available.
  • A working tracking link.
  • An estimated delivery date only when reliable information is available.
  • Support contact details for questions.

Send the email after the shipment is confirmed in your fulfilment system. Do not tell customers an item has shipped merely because stock has arrived or a shipping label has been created.

Subject: Your Backordered Item Has Shipped — Order #[Order Number]

Hi [Customer Name],

Good news! The backordered [Product Name] from order #[Order Number] has shipped.

Shipment date: [Confirmed Shipment Date]
Shipping carrier: [Carrier Name]
Tracking number: [Tracking Number]
Estimated delivery: [Estimated Date, if confirmed]

You can check the latest shipment information here: Track Your Package

Thank you for your patience while we waited for this item to become available. If you have questions about your shipment, contact us at [Support Email].

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Customer Support Team]
[Company Name]

Replace every placeholder before sending. If tracking is not available yet, explain when it is expected to become available rather than inventing a tracking number or link.

1. What Is a Backorder Shipping Confirmation Email?

A backorder shipping confirmation email tells a customer that an item that was previously waiting for replenishment has now shipped. It closes the communication gap between waiting for stock and receiving the package.

This email is different from a backorder update. An update explains the current availability situation while the customer is still waiting. A shipping confirmation should report an actual shipment event and provide useful tracking details when possible.

It also differs from an ordinary order confirmation, which normally confirms that the business received the order. A backorder shipping confirmation should specifically identify the delayed item so the customer understands what has now been sent.

If other products from the same order have already shipped, or will ship separately, make that distinction clear. Customers should not have to guess whether a package contains the entire order or only the previously delayed item.

2. Backorder Shipping Confirmation Email Subject Lines

Choose a subject line that communicates the shipment clearly.

  • Your Backordered Item Has Shipped — Order #[Order Number]
  • Good News: [Product Name] Is on Its Way
  • Shipping Confirmation for Order #[Order Number]
  • Your [Product Name] Has Shipped
  • Tracking Information for Your Backordered Item
  • An Update: Your Delayed Item Is on Its Way
  • Your Order #[Order Number] Has a New Shipment
  • Track Your Recently Shipped [Product Name]

Avoid saying “Your Entire Order Has Shipped” when only the backordered item or part of the order has been sent. The subject line should match the actual shipment.

3. How to Include Tracking Information

Tracking information is often the most useful part of a shipping confirmation email. It helps customers monitor the package without contacting support for routine status checks.

Include these details when they are available and verified:

  • Carrier: The shipping company handling the package.
  • Tracking number: The identifier assigned to the shipment.
  • Tracking link: A working URL that opens the correct shipment.
  • Shipment date: The date the package actually left the fulfilment process.
  • Delivery estimate: The carrier's latest estimate, when available.

Make the tracking link easy to find

Place the tracking link near the top or middle of the message. A descriptive link such as “Track Your Package” is easier to understand than a long, unexplained URL.

Explain tracking delays when necessary

Some carriers take time to display their first tracking scan. If your carrier confirms that tracking may take time to update, explain that clearly. Do not promise a specific activation time unless you have reliable information.

Important: Test the tracking link before sending. A correct tracking number paired with a broken or unrelated URL creates unnecessary confusion.

4. Template When Tracking Information Is Not Available Yet

If the item has shipped but the carrier has not provided a usable tracking link, confirm only what your system can verify. Explain how and when the customer can expect further information.

Template: Tracking pending

Subject: Your Backordered Item Has Shipped

Hi [Customer Name],

We are pleased to let you know that [Product Name] from order #[Order Number] has shipped.

The carrier's tracking information is not available in our system yet. We are checking for the tracking details and will share them when they become available.

You can contact us at [Support Email] if you have questions about this shipment.

Thank you for your patience while we arranged the shipment of your backordered item.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

Use this template only if shipment is confirmed. If the package has not yet been handed to the carrier, send a preparation or dispatch update instead.

5. Template for a Partial Shipment

When an order contains several items, the backordered product may ship separately from the items that were available earlier. Make the contents of each shipment clear.

Template: One item shipped separately

Subject: One More Item from Order #[Order Number] Has Shipped

Hi [Customer Name],

We have good news about your order #[Order Number]. The previously backordered [Product Name] has now shipped.

This shipment contains [Product Name]. The other items in your order are [insert their verified status].

Carrier: [Carrier Name]
Tracking number: [Tracking Number]
Tracking link: Track This Shipment

Because the items are shipping separately, they may arrive at different times. Check the tracking information for the latest status of this package.

Thank you for your patience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]

Replace the bracketed text with the actual order and shipment details. Do not state that other items are on their way unless their status has been confirmed.

6. How to Communicate Delivery Estimates

Customers naturally want to know when their delayed item will arrive. Include a delivery estimate when reliable carrier information is available, but distinguish an estimate from a guaranteed date.

When a delivery estimate is available

State the date or delivery window supplied by your shipping system. If it is an estimate, label it as such.

When the carrier has not provided a date

Tell the customer that the delivery estimate is not yet available and direct them to the tracking page for the latest status when tracking is working.

When delivery is delayed after shipment

If you learn that a package is delayed, send a separate delivery update when appropriate. Do not keep repeating the original estimate after it has become outdated.

A shipping confirmation reports dispatch. It should not be treated as proof that the package has already been delivered.

7. How to Automate Backorder Shipping Confirmations

An email automation can send a shipping confirmation when your order system records a confirmed shipment. The trigger should reflect a real fulfilment event rather than a calendar date or a change in inventory alone.

  1. Identify the backordered item. Keep its order number and product details associated with the order.
  2. Monitor the fulfilment status. Wait for the shipping event to be confirmed by your order or fulfilment system.
  3. Retrieve tracking details. Include the carrier, tracking number, and valid link when available.
  4. Build the message from verified data. Personalize the email with the actual product and order information.
  5. Send the confirmation. Trigger it when the shipment event is recorded.
  6. Handle missing data safely. If tracking is not yet available, use a suitable message or send a later tracking update.
  7. Prevent duplicate messages. Check whether a confirmation has already been sent for the same shipment.

Test these situations before launch

  • A backordered item ships in a separate package.
  • The tracking number arrives after the shipment event.
  • One order has multiple shipments.
  • A shipment status is corrected or cancelled.
  • The customer has already received a confirmation for the same shipment.

These checks help prevent false shipping notices, broken tracking links, and repeated emails.

8. Common Backorder Shipping Confirmation Mistakes

1. Sending confirmation before shipment

Stock arriving at a warehouse does not necessarily mean the item has shipped. Use the correct fulfilment status.

2. Forgetting the tracking link

When tracking is available, make it easy for customers to find. If it is not available yet, explain that clearly.

3. Claiming the entire order has shipped

If only one backordered item was dispatched, state that only that item has shipped.

4. Giving an unsupported delivery promise

A carrier estimate can change. Avoid promising a guaranteed delivery date unless your business can genuinely guarantee it.

5. Reusing an outdated tracking number

Check that the tracking information belongs to the specific package being described in the email.

6. Sending duplicate confirmations

Configure the automation to recognize each shipment so customers do not receive multiple identical notices for the same event.

7. Forgetting the customer's earlier wait

A short thank-you for the customer's patience acknowledges the delay and makes the message feel more considerate without distracting from the shipment details.

9. Backorder Shipping Confirmation Checklist

  • The shipment is confirmed in the fulfilment system.
  • The order number and product name are correct.
  • The message identifies which item has shipped.
  • The shipment date is accurate, if included.
  • The carrier and tracking number are correct, if available.
  • The tracking link opens the correct shipment.
  • The delivery estimate is supported by available information.
  • Remaining items are described accurately, if relevant.
  • The customer has a working support contact.
  • The message is readable on mobile devices.
  • The automation prevents duplicate confirmations.
  • No delivery or tracking promises are invented.

Final Thoughts

A useful backorder shipping confirmation tells customers exactly what has shipped and helps them follow the package. The most important details are the affected product, accurate shipment status, tracking information when available, and a clear way to get help.

Send the message only after shipment is confirmed, keep delivery estimates realistic, and explain separate shipments clearly. These practices help customers move from waiting for stock to tracking their order with greater confidence.
