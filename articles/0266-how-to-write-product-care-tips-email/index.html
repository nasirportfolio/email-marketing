How to Write a Product Care Tips Email

A product care tips email gives customers practical advice for looking after something they have already purchased. It can explain cleaning, storage, handling, maintenance, charging, or other product-specific care steps.

The purpose is simple: help the customer use and maintain the product with more confidence. A useful care email should feel like customer support and education, not like an immediate sales pitch.

Quick Answer

To write a product care tips email, identify the product, lead with the most important care advice, explain what to do and what to avoid, and give customers an easy way to find additional help.

Keep the instructions specific to the product. Use short sections or bullet points so customers can quickly find the information they need.

Product Care Tips Email Template

Subject: Simple care tips for your [Product Name]

Hi [First Name],

We hope you're enjoying your [Product Name].

Here are a few simple tips to help you care for it:

  • Clean: [Recommended cleaning method]
  • Store: [Recommended storage method]
  • Handle: [Important handling instruction]
  • Avoid: [Condition or action that could damage the product]
  • Maintain: [Any recommended maintenance]

For more detailed instructions, see [Care Guide or Product Instructions].

If you have any questions, contact us at [Support Email].

Thanks again for choosing [Brand Name].

Best,
[Brand Name] Customer Care

What Is a Product Care Tips Email?

A product care tips email is a post-purchase email that teaches customers how to protect, maintain, clean, store, or otherwise care for a product.

It is different from a product care reminder because the emphasis is on practical advice rather than simply reminding the customer that care is necessary.

For example, a clothing retailer might explain washing and storage instructions. A kitchenware company might explain cleaning and drying. An electronics company might provide charging, storage, and handling guidance.

Why Product Care Tips Are Useful

Customers may know what a product does without knowing the best way to care for it. A short, relevant email can fill that information gap.

1. It gives customers practical help

Instead of making customers search through a long manual or website, the email can put the most important instructions directly in front of them.

2. It supports a better post-purchase experience

The relationship with a customer does not end when the order is delivered. Helpful product education can continue that relationship after the transaction.

3. It can reduce avoidable confusion

Clear instructions can answer common questions before customers need to contact support.

4. It keeps the message relevant to the purchase

A care email is naturally connected to something the customer already bought. This makes it more useful than an unrelated promotional message.

5. It can support long-term customer relationships

Useful post-purchase communication can show that the brand continues to support the customer after payment. Shopify's current guidance also recommends relevant post-purchase education and product-care information as part of customer engagement. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

What to Include in the Email

A good product care email should answer the customer's most practical questions without becoming a complete instruction manual.

Product identification

Clearly identify the product or product category. This is especially important if your customer purchased several products.

The most important care action

Put the most important instruction near the top. If there is one mistake customers should avoid, make that information easy to find.

Cleaning instructions

If cleaning is relevant, explain the appropriate method. Include important restrictions when they are supported by the actual product documentation.

Storage guidance

Explain how the product should be stored when storage conditions matter.

What to avoid

Customers often benefit from knowing what not to do. Examples could include avoiding excessive moisture, high heat, unsuitable cleaning products, or improper handling—but only when those warnings actually apply to the product.

Additional help

Link to the relevant product guide, help page, or support channel when customers may need more detailed information.

How to Write a Product Care Tips Email Step by Step

Step 1: Start with the customer's product

Begin with the product the customer purchased. Avoid making the customer guess which item the instructions are about.

Step 2: Choose three to five useful tips

Select the instructions that are most useful for normal ownership. You do not need to reproduce every line from a product manual.

Step 3: Put the highest-priority tip first

Readers may scan the email rather than read every word. Lead with the action that matters most.

Step 4: Use simple language

Write as though you are explaining the care instructions to a customer who has never used the product before.

Step 5: Add a detailed resource when needed

If the product has complex maintenance requirements, provide a link to the full instructions rather than placing everything inside the email.

Step 6: End with support information

Tell customers where they can ask questions. A care email should make it easier—not harder—to get help.

Product Care Tips Email Examples

Example 1: Clothing Product

Subject: A few care tips for your new sweater

Hi Sarah,

We hope you're enjoying your new sweater. Here are a few simple tips for caring for it:

  • Check the care label before washing.
  • Use the recommended washing method.
  • Avoid care products that are not suitable for the fabric.
  • Follow the recommended drying instructions.
  • Store it according to the garment's care instructions.

If you have questions about caring for your sweater, our customer care team is happy to help.

Example 2: Kitchen Product

Subject: How to care for your new kitchen tool

Hi James,

Thanks again for your purchase. To keep your new kitchen tool in good condition:

  • Clean it using the recommended method.
  • Dry it thoroughly after cleaning when required.
  • Avoid unsuitable cleaning products.
  • Store it in a clean, dry location.

Please check the product instructions for any product-specific care requirements.

Example 3: Electronics Product

Subject: Simple care tips for your new device

Hi Alex,

Now that your device has arrived, here are a few reminders for everyday care:

  • Use the charging method recommended by the manufacturer.
  • Keep the device in appropriate storage conditions.
  • Keep ports and openings clean according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Avoid conditions that the product documentation identifies as unsafe.

For detailed safety and maintenance information, please refer to the manufacturer's documentation.

Example 4: Furniture Product

Subject: Keep your new furniture looking its best

Hi Taylor,

We hope you're enjoying your new furniture. Here are a few simple care reminders:

  • Use the cleaning method recommended for the material.
  • Wipe spills promptly when appropriate for the surface.
  • Avoid cleaning products that are not recommended for the finish.
  • Follow the manufacturer's guidance for long-term care.

If you need help with your product, contact our support team.

Product Care Subject Line Examples

The subject line should make the purpose of the email clear and should accurately represent what is inside.

  • Simple care tips for your [Product Name]
  • How to care for your new [Product Name]
  • A few tips for caring for your [Product Name]
  • Keep your [Product Name] in good condition
  • Important care tips for your [Product Name]
  • Getting the most from your [Product Name]
  • Save these tips for caring for your [Product Name]
  • Your quick [Product Name] care guide

Avoid exaggerated urgency if there is no genuine reason for it. A straightforward subject line often works well for an educational post-purchase message.

When to Send Product Care Tips

Timing should depend on the product and when the customer is likely to need the information.

Immediately after delivery

This can work for products that require important care or setup information right away.

After initial use

Some products make more sense after the customer has had an opportunity to try them. A care email can then address the next practical step.

Before a predictable maintenance task

If a product has a known maintenance cycle, consider sending useful guidance before that task becomes necessary.

As part of a post-purchase sequence

Product education can fit naturally between delivery communication and later messages such as review requests or replenishment reminders. How to Personalize Product Care Emails

Product care instructions are most useful when they match the product the customer actually purchased.

Segment by product

A customer who purchased a leather product may need different instructions from someone who purchased an electronic device.

Segment by product category

If you sell many individual products, grouping them into care categories can make automation easier.

Use purchase history carefully

Purchase history can help determine which care content is relevant. Shopify recommends using customer profiles and purchase history to personalize post-purchase communication. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Exclude irrelevant customers

If a customer returned or cancelled the product, make sure your automation does not continue sending care instructions that no longer apply.

How to Automate Product Care Emails

Product care emails can be automated when your email platform receives the necessary customer and purchase information.

Step 1: Identify the trigger

Choose a relevant event such as purchase, fulfillment, delivery, or another product-related customer event.

Step 2: Connect the product information

The automation should know which product or category the customer purchased so that the correct care content is sent.

Step 3: Create the care email

Write a concise version of the care instructions and include a link to a more detailed guide if needed.

Step 4: Add appropriate delays

The delay should match the product's customer journey. Do not use the same timing for every product category.

Step 5: Add exclusion rules

Consider excluding customers whose orders were cancelled, refunded, or returned.

Step 6: Review performance

Monitor relevant email engagement and customer-support feedback. If customers continue asking questions that the email should answer, improve the content.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Using generic advice

"Take good care of your product" does not tell the customer what to do. Give specific, accurate instructions.

2. Making unsupported claims

Do not promise that a particular care method will make a product last a certain number of years unless you have reliable evidence for that claim.

3. Copying instructions from another product

Care requirements can vary significantly between materials and products. Use the actual manufacturer's or brand's instructions.

4. Making the email too long

Put the most useful information in the email and move detailed documentation to a dedicated help page when appropriate.

5. Turning every care email into a sales pitch

The primary purpose should be customer education. If you include a recommendation, it should be genuinely relevant to the product and customer.

6. Ignoring mobile readers

Use short paragraphs, clear headings, and bullet points so the customer can scan the message easily on a phone.

Product Care Email Checklist

Before sending your product care tips email, check:

  • ☐ The correct product is identified.
  • ☐ The care instructions are accurate.
  • ☐ The most important tip appears early.
  • ☐ Cleaning guidance is included when relevant.
  • ☐ Storage guidance is included when relevant.
  • ☐ Important things to avoid are clearly explained.
  • ☐ The language is simple and practical.
  • ☐ A detailed help resource is provided when necessary.
  • ☐ Customer support information is easy to find.
  • ☐ The timing matches the product's customer journey.
  • ☐ Returned or cancelled orders are handled appropriately.
  • ☐ The email is easy to read on mobile devices.
  • ☐ Links point to the correct current pages.
  • ☐ No unsupported product claims are included.

Final Thoughts

A product care tips email is a practical way to help customers after a purchase. It can answer common questions, make product ownership easier, and show that your brand continues to provide value after checkout.

The key is relevance. Give customers advice that actually applies to the product they purchased, put the most important information near the beginning, and make additional help easy to find.

When product care communication is useful rather than promotional, it becomes a natural part of the post-purchase customer experience.
