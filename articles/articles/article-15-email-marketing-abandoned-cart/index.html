Email Marketing Abandoned Cart Emails: A Complete Beginner's Guide

Learn how abandoned cart emails can remind customers about products they considered buying and help businesses create useful automated email marketing experiences.

What Is an Abandoned Cart Email?

An abandoned cart email is an automated email sent to a potential customer who added products to an online shopping cart but did not complete the purchase.

The email gives the customer a reminder about the products they left behind and can provide a convenient way to return to the shopping experience.

Why Are Abandoned Cart Emails Important?

Customers may leave an online store without completing a purchase for many different reasons. They may become distracted, need more time to decide, encounter unexpected costs, or simply forget to return to the store.

An abandoned cart email provides an opportunity to reconnect with customers who have already shown interest in particular products.

  • Remind customers about products in their cart
  • Bring interested shoppers back to the store
  • Provide a convenient return path to the cart
  • Support automated customer communication
  • Improve the overall shopping experience

How Does an Abandoned Cart Email Work?

  1. A customer visits an online store.
  2. The customer adds one or more products to a cart.
  3. The customer leaves without completing the purchase.
  4. The store identifies the eligible abandoned cart.
  5. An automated email is sent according to the workflow settings.
  6. The customer can return to the cart and continue shopping.

1. Send a Helpful Reminder

The primary purpose of an abandoned cart email is to remind the customer about the unfinished shopping experience.

Example subject line:
Did You Forget Something?

The message should be helpful rather than aggressive. A simple reminder can be enough for customers who intended to return.

2. Show the Products Left in the Cart

When possible, display the products associated with the abandoned cart. Product names, images, prices, and relevant information can help customers recognize what they were considering.

Showing the actual products makes the reminder more useful than sending a generic promotional email.

3. Use a Clear Call to Action

The customer should have an obvious way to return to the shopping cart.

Example:
Return to Your Cart

Keep the primary action clear and easy to understand.

4. Write a Relevant Subject Line

The subject line should accurately reflect the purpose of the email. Avoid misleading messages simply to increase opens.

Examples:
  • Your Cart Is Waiting
  • Still Thinking It Over?
  • You Left Something Behind
  • Complete Your Purchase When You're Ready

5. Keep the Email Simple

An abandoned cart email usually has one primary goal: help the customer return to the cart.

Avoid filling the message with unrelated information that distracts from the main action.

6. Consider Customer Concerns

Some customers abandon carts because they have questions about shipping, returns, payment methods, product details, or other parts of the purchasing process.

Where appropriate, an abandoned cart email can provide useful information that helps customers make a more informed decision.

Tip: Use accurate information about shipping, returns, pricing, and product availability. Never make promises that the store cannot fulfill.

7. Use Abandoned Cart Automation

Automation allows businesses to send abandoned cart emails based on customer activity rather than manually sending each message.

A workflow can use an abandoned-cart event as a trigger and then send one or more messages according to the campaign rules.

Abandoned Cart Email Sequence

Email 1: Friendly Reminder

Remind the customer about the products left in the cart and provide a clear link back to the shopping cart.

Email 2: Helpful Information

Provide useful information that may help answer common customer questions about the purchase.

Email 3: Final Reminder

Send a final relevant reminder when appropriate without creating false urgency.

When Should an Abandoned Cart Email Be Sent?

The timing of an abandoned cart email depends on the business, customer behavior, product type, and overall campaign strategy.

Some businesses may send an initial reminder relatively soon after abandonment, followed by additional messages if appropriate.

The best timing should be evaluated through testing and campaign performance rather than assumed to be identical for every business.

Should Every Abandoned Cart Receive an Email?

Not necessarily. Businesses should consider their privacy practices, consent requirements, customer expectations, and applicable laws when designing abandoned cart campaigns.

The workflow should also avoid repeatedly contacting customers who have already completed their purchase or otherwise become ineligible for the campaign.

Personalization in Abandoned Cart Emails

Personalization can make an abandoned cart reminder more relevant when accurate information is available.

For example, an email can refer to the products associated with the customer's cart instead of using a generic message.

Example:
Your selected products are still waiting in your cart.

Using Discounts in Abandoned Cart Emails

Some businesses choose to offer a discount as part of an abandoned cart strategy. However, discounts should not automatically be offered to every customer.

Businesses should consider their margins, customer behavior, brand positioning, and campaign goals before using discounts.

Tip: Do not train customers to abandon carts simply because they expect a discount afterward.

Mobile-Friendly Abandoned Cart Emails

Many customers shop and read email on smartphones. Abandoned cart emails should therefore be easy to read and interact with on smaller screens.

  • Use readable text
  • Keep important information easy to find
  • Use buttons that are comfortable to tap
  • Make product images responsive
  • Test the email on mobile devices

Common Abandoned Cart Email Mistakes

  • Sending irrelevant reminders
  • Using misleading subject lines
  • Making the call to action difficult to find
  • Sending too many messages
  • Ignoring mobile users
  • Failing to stop the workflow after purchase
  • Using false urgency
  • Offering unnecessary discounts
  • Ignoring customer privacy and consent requirements
  • Failing to test the complete automation

Abandoned Cart Email Best Practices

  1. Keep the message relevant to the abandoned cart.
  2. Use a clear subject line.
  3. Show useful product information.
  4. Include one primary call to action.
  5. Make the email mobile-friendly.
  6. Use automation carefully.
  7. Stop reminders after a completed purchase.
  8. Respect customer privacy and applicable requirements.
  9. Test different timing and messaging approaches.
  10. Review campaign performance regularly.

How to Create an Abandoned Cart Email Campaign

  1. Define the goal of the campaign.
  2. Identify the abandoned-cart event or trigger.
  3. Decide which customers are eligible.
  4. Create the email content.
  5. Write a clear subject line.
  6. Add a relevant call to action.
  7. Set the workflow timing.
  8. Create conditions that prevent unnecessary messages.
  9. Test the complete workflow.
  10. Monitor performance and improve the campaign.

How to Measure Abandoned Cart Email Performance

Businesses can evaluate abandoned cart campaigns using measurements such as delivered emails, opens, clicks, completed purchases, revenue attributed to the campaign, and unsubscribe activity.

The most useful measurements depend on the campaign objective and the tracking capabilities of the email marketing platform.

Conclusion

Abandoned cart emails can help businesses reconnect with customers who leave an online shopping cart without completing their purchase.

An effective campaign should be relevant, clear, useful, and respectful of customer expectations. Automation can make abandoned cart communication more consistent while reducing manual work.

Start with a simple reminder, use a clear call to action, test the timing and messaging, and review campaign performance to improve the strategy over time.
