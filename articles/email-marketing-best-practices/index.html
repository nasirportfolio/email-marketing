Email Marketing Best Practices: A Beginner's Guide

Learn practical email marketing best practices for creating useful, relevant, mobile-friendly, and effective email campaigns.

What Are Email Marketing Best Practices?

Email marketing best practices are guidelines that help marketers create useful, relevant, and responsible email campaigns.

Following good practices can help businesses communicate more effectively with subscribers while building long-term relationships with their audience.

1. Build a Permission-Based Email List

Start with people who have given appropriate permission to receive marketing emails from your business.

A quality email list is more valuable than a large list containing people who are not interested in receiving your messages.

2. Provide Useful Content

Every email should have a clear purpose and provide something useful to the recipient.

Useful content can include educational information, helpful resources, product information, company updates, or relevant offers.

3. Write Clear Subject Lines

The subject line gives subscribers an immediate idea of what the email contains.

Keep subject lines clear, relevant, and accurate. Avoid misleading claims simply to increase opens.

4. Keep Your Email Focused

Avoid trying to communicate too many unrelated ideas in one email. A focused message makes it easier for subscribers to understand the main purpose of the campaign.

Use a Clear Call to Action

A call to action tells the reader what to do next. Examples include reading an article, visiting a product page, registering for an event, or requesting more information.

5. Make Emails Mobile-Friendly

Many people read emails on smartphones and other mobile devices. Your email should therefore be easy to read and interact with on smaller screens.

  • Use readable text sizes.
  • Keep paragraphs reasonably short.
  • Use buttons or links that are easy to tap.
  • Check the layout on different screen sizes.
  • Avoid unnecessarily wide content.

6. Use Segmentation

Email segmentation means dividing subscribers into groups based on useful characteristics, interests, behavior, or engagement.

Segmentation can help marketers send more relevant content instead of sending exactly the same message to every subscriber.

7. Personalize When Appropriate

Personalization can make email communication more relevant when the necessary information has been collected appropriately.

Personalization may include using a subscriber's name or delivering content based on their interests, previous actions, or preferences.

8. Test Your Emails Before Sending

Always review an email before sending it to your audience. Testing can help identify problems before they affect subscribers.

  • Check spelling and grammar.
  • Test links and buttons.
  • Review images and layout.
  • Check the email on mobile devices.
  • Confirm the correct audience is selected.
  • Review the subject line and sender information.

9. Use Email Automation Carefully

Automation can save time by sending messages based on predefined triggers and conditions.

Common automated workflows include welcome emails, abandoned cart reminders, post-purchase messages, and re-engagement campaigns.

Automated emails should remain relevant and should not overwhelm subscribers with unnecessary messages.

10. Monitor Email Marketing Metrics

Measuring campaign performance helps marketers understand how subscribers interact with their emails.

  • Delivery results
  • Click-through rate
  • Conversion rate
  • Bounce rate
  • Unsubscribe rate

Use performance information to identify areas for improvement rather than relying only on assumptions.

11. Keep Your Email List Healthy

Regularly review your audience and follow appropriate list management practices.

Pay attention to inactive subscribers, bounced addresses, and unsubscribe requests. Good list management can improve the quality of your email marketing efforts.

12. Respect Subscriber Preferences

Subscribers should be able to manage their email preferences appropriately and unsubscribe from marketing communications when they no longer want to receive them.

Respecting subscriber choices is an important part of building trust and maintaining responsible email marketing practices.

Common Email Marketing Mistakes

  • Sending emails without permission
  • Sending irrelevant content
  • Using misleading subject lines
  • Ignoring mobile users
  • Sending too many emails
  • Using unclear calls to action
  • Failing to test emails
  • Ignoring campaign performance
  • Failing to respect unsubscribe requests

How Beginners Can Improve Their Email Marketing

  1. Build a permission-based audience.
  2. Choose a clear campaign goal.
  3. Create useful and relevant content.
  4. Write a clear subject line.
  5. Add a focused call to action.
  6. Make the email mobile-friendly.
  7. Test the email before sending.
  8. Measure the results.
  9. Use the results to improve future campaigns.
