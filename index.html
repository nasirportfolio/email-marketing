About This Resource

Email marketing involves much more than sending promotional emails. Successful campaigns require useful content, clear communication, audience understanding, segmentation, automation, testing, and measurement.

This resource focuses on practical explanations and examples so that readers can understand individual concepts and apply them to their own projects.
