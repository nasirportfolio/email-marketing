Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Nasir Email Marketing is an educational website operated by Muhammad Nasir Uddin. This Privacy Policy describes information that may be processed when you visit or use this website.

The website uses Google Analytics and includes Google AdSense advertising code. Some pages may also contain affiliate links to third-party products or services.

This policy should be read together with the relevant notices and privacy controls provided by third-party services. Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, this policy alone does not replace the required consent.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on how you access the website and which services operate on a page, information that may be processed includes:

  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • IP address and approximate geographic information.
  • Pages viewed and interactions with website content.
  • Visit timestamps, referral sources, and usage information.
  • Cookie identifiers and similar online identifiers.
  • Information you choose to provide when contacting the publisher.

The precise information collected depends on the services used, their settings, your device and browser, and your applicable privacy choices. Do not send sensitive personal information through ordinary email unless it is necessary and you understand the associated risks.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through this website and its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Understand traffic and how visitors use pages.
  • Identify technical issues and improve website performance.
  • Measure advertising delivery and performance.
  • Support affiliate referral attribution where applicable.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and correction requests.
  • Meet applicable legal, security, and compliance obligations.

4. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure website traffic and understand how visitors interact with its content. Depending on the configuration, Google Analytics may process information such as page views, device and browser information, approximate location, referral sources, and online identifiers.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Analytics information helps the publisher understand website usage and identify areas for improvement.

Google processes information according to its own policies and the applicable service settings. For more information, read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store in a browser. Similar technologies can also be used to recognize browsers, measure activity, or support service functionality.

Depending on the services and settings in use, cookies or similar technologies may support analytics, advertising, frequency capping, reporting, referral attribution, and other functions.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain website or third-party service features. Browser settings do not necessarily replace consent choices required by applicable law.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

This website includes Google AdSense advertising code. When ads are served, Google and other third-party advertising providers may process information through cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser identifiers, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to deliver advertisements, limit repeated displays, measure advertising performance, prevent abuse, and, depending on the user's settings and applicable choices, personalize advertisements.

Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website or other websites.

You can manage Google's advertising personalization settings through Google Ads Settings . You can also find information about online advertising choices at YourAdChoices .

These settings may affect personalized advertising but do not necessarily stop all advertisements or all data processing. Other advertising providers may have their own privacy notices and opt-out tools.

For more information about Google's advertising technologies, visit Google's advertising technologies information .
